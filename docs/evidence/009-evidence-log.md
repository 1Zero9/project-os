# Experiment 009 — Early Structural-Proof Evidence Log

**Status:** Interim evidence only. This is not an end-to-end delivery result
and does not promote v0.2.

## Evidence case

Vecta was used as a bounded, unrelated structural-proof case. The founder's
authoritative direction was that Vecta should be a quiet, sensible guide to a
new job, with the founder as the initial candidate. The purpose was to test the
compact v0.2 loop through the first founder-visible proof, not to make Vecta a
Project OS priority or to complete a Vecta feature.

## What occurred

1. The direction was reduced to a concise delivery brief.
2. The proof used existing Vecta profile-completion, saved-role and pipeline
   state; it added no AI service, persistence, account change, job ingestion,
   deployment or external data action.
3. A committed local proof selected one calm next action and stated that Vecta
   assists rather than makes career decisions.
4. Its focused tests passed and its production build passed. Vecta's targeted
   lint command remained blocked by a pre-existing hydration-effect error in
   `app/page.tsx`, outside the proof's changes.
5. The founder then explicitly directed attention back to Project OS rather
   than expanding Vecta.

## Confirmed signals

- A short authority-owned brief was sufficient to make a bounded product choice.
- The proof reached a reviewable, committed state without adding the candidate
  controls that the v0.2 simplification deliberately excludes.
- Existing product state was enough to form a limited, explainable next-action
  suggestion.
- Scope remained contained after the founder reasserted Project OS as the
  priority.

## What this does not establish

- Founder acceptance of a finished Vecta feature.
- That the guidance reduces candidate uncertainty or improves job-search
  outcomes.
- A direct-delivery time/cost comparison.
- An end-to-end PASS under Experiment 009's classification criteria.
- The 80% idea-to-project ambition.

## Classification

**STRUCTURAL-PROOF STAGE COMPLETED / NO DELIVERY CLASSIFICATION.**

The result is evidence that the compact loop can reach its first useful
checkpoint. It is not evidence that the loop has delivered an accepted outcome
or outperformed direct delivery.

## Reusable learning

Treat the founder-visible structural proof as an early decision point, not a
reason to keep implementing. When the product/project ceases to be the
founder's priority, stop cleanly and retain the bounded evidence.
